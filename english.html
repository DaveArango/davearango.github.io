02 · English learning at SENA

Practice makes learning visible.

I am currently in the fourth level of a six-level English learning process at SENA. Since I had studied English before, these activities have been especially useful as a way to reactivate previous knowledge, discover new concepts and, above all, practice.

04/06
My current level

Fourth level in a six-level learning path, with a focus on practice and communication.

Three activities

Activities that have been useful to me.

These activities have not only tested what I know. They have given me opportunities to use English in different formats and to notice which skills need more practice.

Activity 01

Forms and quizzes

Knowledge tests and online forms have helped me review vocabulary, grammar and concepts in a structured way. They are useful because they provide a concrete result that allows me to identify what I remember and what I need to review.

One example from my learning process shows a score of 91.42/100. For me, the value was not only the grade, but the opportunity to check my understanding and reinforce previous knowledge.

Activity 02

Mind maps

Mind maps have helped me organize ideas visually instead of studying isolated words or rules. They make connections between resources, goals, strategies and the environment where I use English.

The example below represents my Personal Learning Environment (PLE), connecting digital tools, learning strategies and my professional environment.

Activity 03

Speaking videos

Recording videos has been particularly useful because it forces me to produce English instead of only understanding it. Speaking practice helps me remember structures, improve fluency and become more comfortable expressing ideas.

The example included here represents one of the speaking activities I completed during the course.

Why it helped

Connecting ideas.

The mind map was useful because it transformed several learning elements into a single visual structure. It helped me think about English not only as a subject, but as a tool that can be connected with work, technology, research and communication.

It also made me reflect on how I manage my own learning: setting goals, practicing regularly, using digital resources and checking my progress.

Speaking practice

From understanding to producing.

I already had some previous experience with English, so many activities worked as a refresher. However, speaking tasks exposed the difference between recognizing English and producing it spontaneously.

Recording a video gives me a practical situation in which I have to choose words, build sentences, pronounce them and keep communicating. That makes it valuable practice rather than only a theoretical exercise.

Review and feedback

Measuring what I know.

Forms and quizzes have given my learning process a measurable component. A result can show whether a topic is already familiar or whether it deserves another review.

The three activities complement each other: quizzes help me check knowledge, mind maps help me organize it, and videos help me use it. Together, they turn previous knowledge into active practice.

My reflection

Why these activities matter to me.

The main benefit has been practice. Because I had already studied English, the course has allowed me to remember fundamentals, learn concepts I did not know and keep using the language. The most important challenge for me is turning comprehension into confident production, especially when speaking.
